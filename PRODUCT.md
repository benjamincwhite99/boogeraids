# EveryMind survey dashboard

## Register
product

## Users
EveryMind evaluation and training staff, plus anonymous case competition judges, reviewing an offline survey report on desktop or phone.

## Product Purpose
Turn checked survey exports into understandable findings and feasible pilot decisions. Let staff inspect denominators, audience differences, missing data and uncertainty.

## Brand Personality
Calm, readable and candid. Preserve the existing editorial typography and chart palette documented in README.md.

## Anti-references
Avoid the existing review's identified problems: seven equally weighted findings, eight competing actions, and a session scorecard that implies predictive or trainer-performance validity.

## Design Principles
- Lead with three supported findings and three proposed pilots.
- Put evidence limits beside the claims they qualify.
- Preserve access to detailed comparisons through navigation and disclosure.
- Separate retrospective self-report from demonstrated comprehension.

## Accessibility and Inclusion
Preserve keyboard-accessible chart details, text tables, responsive layouts and color-independent labels. Use clear wording for nontechnical readers.
